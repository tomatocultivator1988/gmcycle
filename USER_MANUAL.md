# GM Cycle User Manual

**For GM Cycle — Cabanatuan City**

---

## How to Open the System

Open Google Chrome or any browser on your phone or computer. Go to your GM Cycle website address.

**Tip:** You can add it to your home screen like an app:
- **On Phone:** Tap the browser menu → "Add to Home screen"
- **On Computer:** Click the install icon in the address bar

---

## The Main Screen (Dashboard)

When you open the system, you'll see the **Dashboard**. It shows you the numbers at a glance:

- How many customers have accounts
- How many are active, overdue, or fully paid
- How much money has been collected today, this week, this month
- How many accounts are past due

**To add a new customer:** Click the **"New Account"** button at the top right.

---

## How to Move Around

Use the bar at the top of the screen (or at the bottom on your phone):

| Button | Goes to | What you can do there |
|--------|---------|----------------------|
| Dashboard | Main screen | See all the numbers |
| Accounts | Customer list | View or add customers |
| Payments | Payment list | See all payments |
| Reports | Report list | Print reports |
| Settings | Configuration | Change penalty/discount amounts |

---

## How to Add a New Customer and Motorcycle

1. Click **"Accounts"** on the menu bar
2. Click the **"New Account"** button
3. Fill out the form:

**Customer Info (left side):**
- Customer's full name
- Customer's phone number
- Customer's complete address

**Motorcycle Info (left side):**
- Brand (e.g., Honda, Yamaha)
- Model (e.g., XRM, Mio)
- Description (color, year, any details)

**Contract Info (right side):**
- **Cash Price** — the price if paid in cash
- **Installment Price** — the price under installment
- **Down Payment** — initial payment
- **Term** — how many months to pay (12, 24, 36, or 48)
- **Start Date** — when the first payment starts
- **Due Day** — what day of the month payment is due (10th, 20th, or 30th)

The system will automatically compute the **Monthly Payment** and **Remaining Balance** for you.

4. Review the numbers
5. Click **"Create Account"**
6. A pop-up will ask "Are you sure?" — click **"Yes, create account"**

---

## How to View a Customer's Account

1. Click **"Accounts"** on the menu
2. Find the customer — you can type their name in the **search bar**
3. Click **"View"** beside their name

You will see:
- Customer info and motorcycle details
- The payment schedule (all their due dates and amounts)
- Their payment history
- Any penalties or discounts
- A **"Post Payment"** button to record a payment

---

## How to Record a Payment

1. Open the customer's account (see above)
2. Click **"Post Payment"**
3. Fill out the pop-up:
   - **Amount Paid** — how much they paid
   - **Payment Date** — what date they paid
   - **Payment Type** — choose one:
     - **Regular** — paying the normal monthly amount
     - **Partial** — paying less than the monthly amount
     - **Advance** — paying ahead of the due date
     - **Full** — paying off everything
   - **Method** — Cash, GCash, or Bank
   - **Cashier** (optional) — your name
   - **Notes** (optional) — any remarks
4. Click **"Post Payment"**
5. A pop-up will ask "Are you sure?" — click **"Yes, post payment"**

**The system will automatically:**
- Deduct the payment from the balance
- Add a ₱200 **penalty** if the payment is 7+ days late
- Give a ₱200 **discount** if paid before the due date
- Update the status (Active, Overdue, Fully Paid, etc.)

## How to Print a Receipt

1. Open the customer's account
2. Scroll down to **Payment History**
3. Click **"Print"** beside the payment you want a receipt for
4. A receipt page will open
5. Press **Ctrl + P** (or tap the printer icon) to print

**To save as a PDF instead of printing:**  
When the print window opens, choose **"Save as PDF"** instead of a printer.

| Penalty Report | All penalties charged |
| Discount Report | All discounts given |
| Outstanding Balances | Customers who still owe money |

## How to Change Settings

Only do this if you need to change the penalty or discount amounts.

1. Click **"Settings"** on the menu
2. You can change:

- **Penalty Amount** — the fee for late payment (default: ₱200)
- **Discount Amount** — the discount for early payment (default: ₱200)
- **Due Day Options** — which days of the month are available (10, 20, 30)

3. Click **"Save Configuration"**
4. Confirm by clicking **"Yes, save changes"**

## Quick Reference: Rules

### Monthly Payment
```
Monthly Payment = (Installment Price − Down Payment) ÷ Number of Months
```

**Example:**
- Motorcycle installment price: ₱96,000
- Down payment: ₱10,000
- Remaining balance: ₱86,000
- Term: 24 months
- Monthly payment: ₱86,000 ÷ 24 = **₱3,583.33**

### Penalty (Late Payment)
- Payment is **7 or more days late** → **₱200 penalty**
- Payment is 1 to 6 days late → No penalty
- Payment is on time → No penalty

### Discount (Early Payment)
- Payment is **before the due date** → **₱200 discount**
- Payment is on the due date → No discount, no penalty
- Payment is after the due date → No discount

### Account Status
| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| ACTIVE | Customer is paying on time |
| DUE TODAY | Payment is due today |
| OVERDUE | Payment is past due |
| FULLY PAID | Customer has paid everything |
